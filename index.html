牛涵 Niu Han

Niu Han is a filmmaker, writer, and tech founder based in Nashville.

His work is informed by two decades of industry practice and academic research across Beijing, Aotearoa, and North America. Having crafted films across four continents — written and directed in solitude — his cinema functions as a dry, observational anatomy of the gap between what we order and what we're actually served.

Through his tech foundry, TARTARY, Han integrates cinematic logic into digital engineering. His latest work, Commandment (2026), continues an ongoing search for a sense of order within the beautifully organized wreckage of modern life.

牛涵是一位现居纳什维尔的电影导演、作家及科技创业者。

他的独特创作视野产生于二十年横跨北京、新西兰与北美的行业实战与学术积累。牛涵编剧执导、独立拍摄的足迹遍布四大洲，冷峻解剖日常生活里那道裂缝——下的单，和上的菜，之间的区别。

通过其科技公司TARTARY，牛涵将电影逻辑引入数字工程。他的最新长片《诫命》（2026）延续他一贯的追问——在这个美丽而混乱的现代废墟里，秩序究竟藏在哪里。
