Writing 写作

牛涵 Niu Han

Niu Han is a filmmaker and contemporary artist whose work explores memory, identity, and the poetics of everyday life. Working across narrative film, documentary, and installation, Han's practice is rooted in observation — the quiet rhythms of human experience, the spaces between words, and the textures of time.

His feature films have screened at international film festivals and art institutions. His latest work, Commandment (2026), continues his exploration of language, faith, and moral ambiguity.

Han lives and works between Beijing and Los Angeles.

牛涵是一位电影导演与当代艺术家，其创作围绕记忆、身份认同与日常生活的诗意展开。横跨叙事电影、纪录片与装置艺术，他的实践植根于观察——人类经验的静谧节奏、言语之间的空隙、以及时间的肌理。

他的长片作品曾在国际电影节与艺术机构展映。最新作品《诫命》（2026）延续了他对语言、信仰与道德模糊性的探索。

牛涵生活和工作于北京与洛杉矶之间。
